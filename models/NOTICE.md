`stop.json` and `stop.tflite`: the "Stop" wake word model (beta 20241017.5) from
[microWakeWord](https://github.com/kahrendt/microWakeWord) by Kevin Ahrendt,
Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE-microWakeWord`).

`hangup_nabu`, `nabu_hangup` and `ok_nabu_hangup` (`.json` and `.tflite`): the "hangup
nabu", "nabu hangup" and "ok nabu hangup" wake word models (V9) from
[Tater-Wake-Words](https://github.com/TaterTotterson/Tater-Wake-Words) by Tater
Totterson, commit af4f03b7075d090d72a66fff4c60712e23a192bb, MIT License
(`LICENSE-TaterWakeWords`). The `.json` files are the upstream `.esphome.json`
manifests, renamed.
